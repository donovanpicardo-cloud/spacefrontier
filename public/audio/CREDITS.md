# Sound effects

Selected recordings from Kenney's **Impact Sounds** (2019) and **Sci-fi Sounds** (2020), licensed CC0 1.0 Universal.

- https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original filenames are retained. The recordings provide cabin footsteps and selected machinery transients. Suit footsteps, motor layers, atmosphere and the score are generated in the game.
